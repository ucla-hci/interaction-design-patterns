## Solution

Link each new or revised item to what produced it. Keep what it replaced.

Variation: a composed item links to all the items that it uses. When the composed item is in focus, mark those items and their links.

## Examples

- HALO edit edge
- PerspectEvolver "why this changed"
- THESEUS replaced hypothesis keeps prior experiments
- IdeaSynth generated node linked to its origin; brief highlights its nodes
