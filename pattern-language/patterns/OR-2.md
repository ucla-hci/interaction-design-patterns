## Solution

1. Link two parts — C-4 Persistent Structure Map, C-8 Confidence Cue
   - *User:* links two parts on the [persistent structure map](C-4).
   - *System:* scores how well the two parts fit. Shows the score on the link as a [confidence
     cue](C-8).
2. Strengthen a link — C-6 Detail on Demand, C-10 Proposal Set
   - *User:* opens the [detail](C-6) of a link, and applies a proposal from the [proposal
     set](C-10).
   - *System:* proposes changes that make the two parts fit better.

## Examples

- *IdeaSynth.* A researcher links nodes to state their logical relation (§4.2). Each edge has a
  colour for the strength of the connection, from red (weak) to green (strong) (§4.2, Fig. 4 ④).
  The edge expands to a suggestion that makes the two nodes more coherent, and the researcher
  can adopt it (§4.1.4, §4.2). In the deployment study, D4 used the edges to check a drafted
  paper, and found the strength ratings useful (§6.1.1).
