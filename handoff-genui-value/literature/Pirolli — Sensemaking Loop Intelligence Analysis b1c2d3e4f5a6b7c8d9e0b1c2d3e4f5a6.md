**Survey** → **Approach type**: process-model taxonomy (sensemaking stages)

# One Sentence
Models expert analysis as two interlocking loops — a foraging loop (find, filter, organize, prioritize information into evidence) and a sensemaking loop (build schemas, hypotheses, and conclusions from evidence) — across sixteen distinct process steps.

---

# More Sentences
Derived from cognitive task analysis of intelligence analysts. Four macro-phases: information gathering → representation → insight development through manipulation → knowledge creation or direct action. Distinguishes bottom-up (data→theory) from top-down (theory→data) traversal.

---

# Key Points

### Tasks compose into loops, not lists
Unlike flat taxonomies, the model says building-block subtasks *chain*: locate feeds comprehend feeds compare feeds act. A task battery that tests purposes in isolation misses the transitions.

### Representation is the leverage point
The model's central claim: the biggest gains come from better *representations* of the information ("representation of information in ways that aid analysis"). This is precisely the GenUI bet — the LLM response as a purpose-built representation rather than undifferentiated prose.

# Take-Away
- Theoretical grounding for why format should matter at all: sensemaking gains come from representation, and GenUI is a representation intervention
- Suggests the task battery include at least one *chained* task (locate→compare→decide) rather than only isolated probes
