# One Sentence
Cross-surface panel study (chat + search + browsing, May 2026, US/GB) finding that AI answers *bifurcate* information seeking: 59.5% of episodes collapse (terminate at the AI response), 31.6% scaffold into multi-step journeys, plus a 41% non-IS "workbench layer" (drafting, coding, editing).

---

# More Sentences
Episodes were reconstructed from time-ordered conversational turns, searches, and pageviews across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Grok. Four episode shapes: Collapsed (59.5%), Scaffolded (31.6%), Redirected (6.4%), Handoff (2.6%). Ask depth (question length), not task type, predicts collapse — collapse is statistically indistinguishable across lookup/learning/comparison episodes. Verification searches occur in ~1% of episodes; median episodes touch zero onward sources.

---

# Key Points

### The answer is the terminal artifact
For ~60% of episodes, the LLM response is where the task *ends* — "the answer itself becomes the evidence surface." Median episodes consult zero further sources.

### The workbench layer
41% of episodes are not information seeking at all but artifact work (drafting, coding, editing) — empirical support for produce as a top-level purpose distinct from consumption.

### Task type doesn't predict episode shape
Marchionini-style categories (lookup/learn/compare) don't differentiate collapse rates; articulation depth does. Caveat for any taxonomy that assumes task type drives behavior.

# Other Notes

# Take-Away
- **Strongest single motivation for the GenUI premise found so far**: if the response terminates the majority of episodes and users verify ~1% of the time, the response's format is the whole game — it is the user's final interface to the task
- Supports produce/act as first-class purposes (the workbench layer) alongside consumption purposes
